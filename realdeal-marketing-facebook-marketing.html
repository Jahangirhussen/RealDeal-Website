What is Facebook Marketing?

Facebook remains one of the most reliable channels for reaching local customers and building a trusted brand presence. We handle everything from setting up and optimizing your business Page to designing scroll-stopping content and running targeted ad campaigns that turn followers into paying customers.

Optimized business Page

A fully set up Page with complete profile, contact and category details that build trust instantly.

On-brand content

Precise ad targeting

Campaigns aimed at the audiences most likely to convert, not just broad reach.

Retargeting ads that bring back visitors and engaged users who didn't convert the first time.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need an existing Facebook Page to start?

No. If you don't have one yet, we'll create and fully set up your business Page as part of onboarding.

Do you write and design the posts yourselves?

Yes, our team handles post design and copywriting so you don't have to create content in-house.

How much do you recommend spending on Facebook Ads?

Budget depends on your goals and market; we typically recommend a starting test budget and scale what performs.

What's the difference between targeting and retargeting?

Targeting reaches new audiences matching your ideal customer profile; retargeting re-engages people who already visited your Page or site.

How often do you post to our Page?

Posting cadence is set in the content calendar, typically several times per week depending on the plan.

Do you manage ad spend directly?

Yes, campaign budgets are managed and optimized by our team within the limits you approve.

How do we track results?

You receive monthly reporting covering reach, engagement, ad performance and lead or sales data.

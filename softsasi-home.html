F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your core competencies in software development?+

Our core competencies in software development include software architecture, design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance. We also provide digital marketing services to help businesses grow.

How do you ensure the confidentiality and security of client data?+

We follow strict data security measures to ensure the confidentiality and integrity of client data, including secure data storage, access control, and disaster recovery plans. We also sign non-disclosure agreements with our clients to protect their data.

What technologies do you specialize in?+

We specialize in a wide range of technologies including Unreal Engine 5, Java, .NET, PHP, Python, React Native, Flutter, and more. We choose the technology based on the project's requirements and feasibility.

What is your software development process?+

We follow the agile software development process, which involves iterative and incremental development. This helps us to adapt to changing requirements and deliver high-quality software.

What is the cost of software development?+

The cost of software development varies depending on the project size, complexity, and technology used. We provide customized solutions and cost estimates after analyzing the project requirements.

What services does your software company provide?+

Our software company provides a range of services including custom software development, web and mobile app development, software testing and quality assurance, software maintenance and support, and digital marketing.

What kind of games do you develop?+

We develop a wide range of games including mobile, console, and PC games. Our games range from casual and puzzle games to action-packed games and RPGs. We also develop multiplayer games with various game modes, online matchmaking, and player statistics.

What game engines do you use for game development?+

We use Unreal Engine 5 to develop games.

How do you ensure engaging gameplay for users?+

We prioritize engaging gameplay and user experience. We involve our target audience early on in the development process and use their feedback to refine our games to ensure that they are easy to play and enjoyable.

What's your payment policy and gateways?+

We require an initial deposit and accept milestone-based payments. Our payment terms are flexible and we offer multiple payment options such as Debit/Credit Card, PayPal, Bkash, Rocket, Nagod, Cryptocurrency, and Cash for secure transactions.

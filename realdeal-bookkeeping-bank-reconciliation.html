Overview

What is Bank Reconciliation?

Your books are only as reliable as they are reconciled. Bank Reconciliation is the careful process of reviewing every bank statement, matching each transaction against your recorded books, and correcting any discrepancy before it snowballs into a bigger problem — so your balances are always accurate and trustworthy.

Bank statement review

Every statement checked line by line against your recorded transactions.

Transaction matching

Deposits, withdrawals and fees matched precisely to your books.

Error correction

Discrepancies, duplicates and missed entries identified and fixed fast.

Fraud detection

Unusual or unauthorized transactions flagged before they become bigger issues.

FAQ

Common questions

How often do you reconcile our accounts?

Monthly is standard, though weekly reconciliation is available for high-volume accounts.

What happens when you find a discrepancy?

We investigate the source, correct the entry in your books and flag it to you if it needs your input.

Can you reconcile multiple bank and card accounts?

Yes, we reconcile every account you connect, however many you have.

Can this catch fraudulent or unauthorized transactions?

Yes, unusual transactions are flagged during the matching process for your review.

How is this service priced?

Pricing is based on the number of accounts and monthly transaction volume being reconciled.

Do you handle reconciliation if our books are already behind?

Yes, we can reconcile backlogged months to bring your accounts current.

What access do you need from us?

Read-only access to your bank and credit card statements or accounts.

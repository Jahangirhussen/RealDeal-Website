FAQ

Common questions

Do we need a business account to start?

No, business account creation is included if you don't already have one.

How many pins do you design each month?

Volume depends on your plan; most accounts publish several new pins per week.

How is board management different from pin design?

Pin design creates the individual graphics; board management organizes and maintains where they live for discoverability.

How long does it take to see traffic results?

Pinterest traffic tends to build gradually and compound over three to six months.

Is Pinterest a good fit for our business?

It works especially well for ecommerce, home, fashion, food and lifestyle brands; we'll advise based on your industry.

Do you also run Pinterest ads?

This service is organic-focused; paid Pinterest campaigns can be scoped separately on request.

What assets do you need from us?

Product photos, blog links or website pages you want pins to drive traffic to.
